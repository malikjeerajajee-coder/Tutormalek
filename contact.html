Common Questions

Questions parents ask before getting in touch

Everything you need to know about contacting me and booking your first session.

How quickly do you reply to WhatsApp messages?
I typically reply within 1–2 hours during UK daytime (9am–9pm GMT). Evenings and weekends may take a little longer, but I always aim to respond the same day. WhatsApp is the fastest way to reach me.
Can I book a trial lesson through the contact form?
Yes — just mention in the message that you'd like a trial session, along with your child's year group and subjects. I'll reply with available time slots within a few hours. Trial lessons are the same price as regular sessions, with no commitment.
What information should I include when I message you?
To help me respond with the most relevant information, please include: your child's year group, the subjects they need help with, their exam board if known (e.g. AQA, Edexcel), and your preferred days/times for lessons. The more detail you share, the more helpful my first reply will be.
Do you offer phone consultations?
Yes. If you prefer a quick phone call to discuss your child's needs before booking, just send me a WhatsApp message first and we can arrange a convenient time to speak. A brief 5–10 minute chat is often the best way to see if we're a good fit.
