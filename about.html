My Story

From Academic Excellence to Dedicated Online Tutor

I know what it takes to achieve top grades, because I've lived it. Throughout school and college, I was consistently a topper in my class, mastering Maths and Science not by rote memorization, but by truly understanding the core concepts. That deep understanding is exactly what I now bring to every single student I teach as a private online tutor.

For the past five years, I've taught over 500 students online across the UK — from ambitious Year 3 children facing their first SATs, to driven Year 11 students aiming for top GCSE grades, to sixth-formers targeting A* at A-Level.

I teach GCSE Maths, Biology, Chemistry, Physics, and English Grammar, plus A-Level Biology, Chemistry, and Physics. I also specialise in 11+ and SATs preparation for grammar school and independent school entrance.

My approach is simple: I teach students the exact study techniques, exam strategies, and conceptual frameworks that helped me top my own classes. No jargon, no rushing, no shame. Every child learns differently, and my job is to help them unlock their own academic potential.

My Approach

My Teaching Philosophy

Every child can succeed. My job is to find the path that works for them.

Personalised for Every Child

No two students are the same. I assess each child's strengths, weaknesses, and learning style, then tailor every lesson to fit them — not the other way around.

Building Confidence First

A confident student is a successful student. I focus on small wins and steady progress so your child believes in their own ability — because once they believe, the grades follow naturally.

Exam Technique Matters

Knowing the content is only half the battle. I teach students how to read questions carefully, manage their time, and structure answers to maximise marks — skills that make the difference between a Grade 5 and a Grade 7.
