My Approach

My teaching philosophy

Every child can succeed. My job is to find the path that works for them.

Personalised for Every Child

No two students are the same. I assess each child's strengths, weaknesses, and learning style, then tailor every lesson to fit them — not the other way around.

Building Confidence First

A confident student is a successful student. I focus on small wins and steady progress so your child believes in their own ability — once they believe, the grades follow naturally.

Exam Technique Matters

Knowing the content is only half the battle. I teach students how to read questions carefully, manage time, and structure answers to maximise marks — skills that make the difference between a Grade 5 and a Grade 7.
