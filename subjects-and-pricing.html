Common Questions

Questions about subjects & pricing

Quick answers to help you choose the right subject and understand how payment works.

Can I mix subjects in one session?
Usually it's best to focus on one subject per session for depth. However, for younger students (Year 6–8) we can split a session between Maths and Science if needed. For GCSE and A-Level, I recommend dedicated sessions to cover content properly.
Do you offer discounts for booking multiple sessions?
The prices are already very competitive. I don't offer bulk discounts because every session is individually prepared and tailored. However, there are no contracts — you can stop anytime without penalty.
What exam boards do you cover for each subject?
I cover all major UK exam boards: AQA, Edexcel, OCR, WJEC, and Eduqas. Each lesson is tailored to the specific syllabus and paper format of your child's board. Just let me know the board when you get in touch.
How do I know which subject to focus on?
If you're unsure, send me a WhatsApp message with your child's recent school reports or a quick description of what they're finding difficult. I'll help you identify the key areas and recommend the best starting subject — no obligation.

Ready to book your first lesson?

Message me on WhatsApp with your child's year group and subjects. I'll respond within hours with available slots and a recommended plan.
