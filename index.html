Questions

Frequently Asked Questions About Online Private Tuition

Quick answers to the most common questions parents ask before booking an online tutor.

Which exam boards do you cover?
I cover all major UK exam boards including AQA, Edexcel, OCR, WJEC, and Eduqas. Every lesson is tailored to the specific syllabus and paper format your child is sitting.
How do online lessons work?
Lessons take place via Zoom or Google Meet. I use a digital whiteboard to explain concepts, share past papers in real time, and record key points for your child to revise later. All you need is a laptop or tablet and a stable internet connection.
Can I book just one session, or do I need a block?
You can book a single session with no commitment. Many parents start with one lesson to see if it's a good fit, then move to weekly sessions. There are no contracts or cancellation fees.
What if my child is in Year 7 or Year 8?
Absolutely — I teach students from Year 3 all the way to Year 13. For Years 7 and 8, I focus on building strong foundations in Maths and Science so your child is confident and prepared when GCSEs begin.
Do you offer lessons during school holidays?
Yes. Holiday intensive sessions are very popular for 11+ prep and GCSE revision. I offer flexible morning and afternoon slots during half-term, Easter, and summer breaks.
How do I pay for lessons?
Payment is simple — I accept bank transfer or PayPal after each session. For regular weekly students, some parents prefer to pay monthly in advance, but weekly payment is perfectly fine too.
