Revision Roadmap

Your GCSE Revision Timeline

Follow this proven 16-week revision plan to stay organised and maximise your GCSE results.

Weeks 1–2

Audit Your Knowledge

Go through each subject specification and rate your confidence on every topic (Red, Amber, Green).

Weeks 3–6

Build Core Knowledge

Start with your weakest topics. Use revision notes and flashcards to build foundational understanding.

Weeks 7–10

Apply With Past Papers

Begin practising with past paper questions topic by topic. Review mark schemes carefully.

Weeks 11–13

Timed Practice & Exam Technique

Move to full timed papers under exam conditions. Practise pacing and command words.

Weeks 14–15

Target Weak Areas

Analyse your timed paper results and go back to weak areas with focused revision.

Week 16

Final Review & Confidence Building

Light revision only — review formula sheets, key definitions, and stay calm.
