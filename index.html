Questions

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to the most common questions parents ask before booking an online tutor.

Which exam boards do you cover?
I cover all major UK exam boards including AQA, Edexcel, OCR, WJEC, and Eduqas. Every lesson is tailored to the specific syllabus and paper format your child is sitting.
How do online lessons work?
Lessons take place via Zoom or Google Meet. I use a digital whiteboard to explain concepts, share past papers in real time, and record key points for your child to revise later.
Can I book just one session?
You can book a single session with no commitment. Many parents start with one lesson to see if it's a good fit, then move to weekly sessions. No contracts or cancellation fees.
What if my child is in Year 7 or 8?
I teach students from Year 3 to Year 13. For Years 7 and 8, I focus on building strong foundations in Maths and Science so your child is confident when GCSEs begin.
Do you offer holiday lessons?
Yes. Holiday intensive sessions are very popular for 11+ prep and GCSE revision. I offer flexible morning and afternoon slots during half-term, Easter, and summer breaks.
How do I pay for lessons?
Payment is simple — I accept bank transfer or PayPal after each session. Weekly payment is perfectly fine, or some parents prefer to pay monthly in advance.
