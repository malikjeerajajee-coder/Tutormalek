Frequently asked questions about GCSE resources

Quick answers about downloads, exam boards and how to use these materials effectively.

Are these GCSE resources really free?
Yes. All past papers, revision notes, worksheets, flashcards and formula sheets on this page are completely free to download. No sign-up or payment is required.
Which exam boards do your GCSE resources cover?
Our GCSE resources cover all major UK exam boards including AQA, Edexcel, OCR, WJEC and Eduqas. Each resource is clearly labelled by board and specification.
Can I use these for Combined Science as well as Separate Sciences?
Yes. Our Biology, Chemistry and Physics resources cover both Combined Science (Trilogy and Synergy) and Separate Science (Triple) specifications. Combined Science topics are marked clearly.
Do you have predicted papers for the 2026 GCSE exams?
Yes. Our predicted papers are written by experienced GCSE tutors using topic trend analysis, examiner reports and advance information patterns. They are updated every exam season.
How should my child use these resources alongside tutoring?
We recommend using revision notes and flashcards for daily recall, topic questions after each tutoring session, and past papers under timed conditions in the final weeks before exams.
